# Product-reduction atoms

Multiplicative reductions of an expression.

## Arguments

- c:

  an expression handle.

## Value

An expression handle.

## Details

- `sd_prod`:

  product of all entries.

- `sd_prod_axis_zero`:

  column-wise products (reduce down rows).

- `sd_prod_axis_one`:

  row-wise products (reduce across columns).
